<h4>The Story Behind Lunches by Lily</h4>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><span>Hello, I'm Lily, the founder!</span></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><span>Lunches By Lily didn't start in a boardroom or with a big business plan.</span></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><span>It started while I was working full-time managing a cafe.</span></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><span>I started cooking meals after work. And w</span><span>hat began as a small side hustle quickly grew through word of mouth. Friends told friends, customers kept coming back and before I knew it, Lunches By Lily had become more than something I did after hours. It had completely overtaken my spare time.</span></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><span>Eventually, I had to make one of the hardest decisions I've ever made: leaving a job I genuinely loved to back myself and pursue Lunches By Lily full-time.</span></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><span>I've never looked back.</span></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><span>Today, every meal is made with the same goal I had when I started: to make eating well simple. Whether you're juggling work, family, training, or just life in general, I want to take one thing off your plate so you have more time for what matters most.</span></p>
<p class="isSelectedEnd"><span>Lunches By Lily isn't just about meal prep. It's about giving people back their time, without compromising on fresh, nutritious food.</span></p>
<p><span>And this is only the beginning &lt;3</span></p>
