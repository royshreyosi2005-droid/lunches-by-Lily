    Frequently asked questions

    How do I order?

    Simply choose your meals (minimum 5), confirm your order and pay to secure your spot for that week.

    All meals are made fresh to order. I don't mass produce or keep stock sitting around.
    What is the minimum order?

    There is a minimum of 5 main meals per order.

    This allows me to keep everything fresh, structured and made-to-order each week.
    When are the meals delivered?

    We deliver across Sydney on Sunday & Wednesday depending on your meals.

    Delivery fees are calculated at checkout based on your location.

    You can also pick up from Auburn 😊

    If your order is across both days, a $6 split delivery fee applies.
    How long do the meals last?

    Meals are made fresh and designed to last up to 7 days refrigerated (for most meals).

    Store them in the fridge (under 5 degrees) as soon as they’re delivered and keep sealed until ready to heat and enjoy.
    Are your meals Halal?

    Yes! I source Halal certified meats where applicable.

    If you have specific dietary requirements, please let me know when ordering so I can confirm suitability.
    Can I customise my order?

    Because meals are prepared in batches each week, full customisation isn’t always possible, but I’ll always try to work with you where I can.
    What if I need to cancel or change my order?

    Orders can be adjusted or cancelled before the weekly cut-off time.

    Once ingredients are purchased and prep has begun, changes may not be possible.

    If something urgent comes up, just reach out as soon as possible and we’ll work through it.
